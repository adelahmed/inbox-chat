1. Reading your mail

  • Folders shows your Outlook folders with the number of unread emails. Click a folder to list its emails; click ▸ to open sub-folders and ⟳ to reload the tree.
  • Messages lists the newest emails first, 50 at a time; scroll down for more. Unread emails are in bold. Use ↑ and ↓ to move through the list.
  • Badges tell you why an email may concern you:
    BadgeMeaning
    To youYou are on the To line.
    CCYou are copied.
    Mentions youThe text mentions you the way people usually address you (set in Profile, e.g. “Dr. Sam”).
    DepartmentIt contains one of your department's keywords or is sent to one of its mailing lists.
    RequestIt asks you for something (a question, an approval, a document).
  • Tick For me above the list to see only emails addressed to you, mentioning you, or for your department.
  • The reading pane shows the email with its formatting, tables and pictures, meeting invitations included. Below it are the attachments and the earlier messages of the same conversation.
  • An email you open is marked as read in Outlook too. You can turn this off in Profile → Learning & behaviour.
  • Open in Outlook opens the email in Outlook's own window, for example to send a draft or to see blocked pictures.

2. Attachments and links

  • Each attachment has an Open button, which opens it in its own application (Word, Excel, PowerPoint…). Pictures, PDF files and text files (TXT, CSV, JSON, XML…) also have Preview, which shows them inside the app.
  • Files opened this way are marked as coming from the internet, so Office opens them in Protected View.
  • Risky file types (programs and scripts such as .exe, .js, .bat) are never opened.
  • Pictures stored on the internet are not downloaded, to protect your privacy (they can tell the sender when you read the email). A note says how many were skipped; open the email in Outlook to see them.
  • Clicking a link asks you first, then opens it in your web browser.

3. The ✨ Assistant panel

When you open an email, the assistant reads it, with its conversation, and shows a panel at the top of the reading pane:

SectionWhat it shows
SummaryA few lines on what the email and its thread are about.
What you need to doThe actions expected from you, or “Nothing for you to do”.
Suggested replyOnly when a reply is needed: a draft in your style, based on how you answered similar emails before. Edit the text freely; with a writing style on, check how the person is addressed in the greeting box.
  • Save to Drafts saves it as a reply; Save as Reply all replies to everyone; Refine in chat moves it to the chat so you can ask for changes (“shorter”, “more formal”, “in English”).
  • Regenerate asks again; Hide folds the panel. Results are kept, so opening the same email again is instant.
  • If you replied or forwarded already, the assistant knows and does not suggest a reply unless something is still open.
Prefer to ask first? Untick Show the summary & suggested reply automatically in Profile → Learning & behaviour. The panel then shows a Summarize & suggest reply button instead. With a cloud provider, this also means an email is sent to the provider only when you click.

4. Chatting with the assistant

Type in the box at the bottom right and press Enter (Shift+Enter for a new line). You can write in English, Arabic or any language your model understands. The assistant looks through your mailbox as needed and shows each step it takes (searching, reading, saving a draft) above its answer.

  • The open email is the default subject. A bar above the chat says Chat is about m12 …: “reply to this”, “who else is involved?” or “translate it” all refer to that email. Click × on the bar to talk about the whole mailbox instead.
  • Answers refer to emails as m12, m13…; click one to open that email.
  • The buttons above an open email start common requests: ✨ Draft reply, ✨ Draft reply all, ✨ Forward (each puts the start of a request in the chat box for you to finish) and ✨ Summarize thread (asks at once).

5. Replies, forwards and new emails

  1. Say what you want. For example “Reply that we agree and will send the file on Sunday”. Short instructions are enough: the assistant reads the thread and writes the full text.
  2. The draft is saved in Outlook. A notice confirms Saved in Outlook Drafts — nothing was sent. Replies keep the conversation history below your text; forwards keep the attachments.
  3. Review and send from Outlook. Open the draft (Drafts folder, or Open in Outlook to edit / send), make any change and press Send there.
Inbox Chat never sends email. There is no send command in the app at all. It also never deletes, moves or flags your emails. The only changes it makes in Outlook are saving drafts and marking emails as read.
  • Meeting invitations are forwarded as a normal email with the invitation attached and its details (subject, time and place) written in the message, so the recipient can read it in any mail program.
  • Your Outlook signature is not added to these drafts (Outlook only adds it in its own editor). Your sign-off from the writing style is.

6. How recipients are found

You can name people the way you speak: “forward to Sara”, “cc Omar Saleh”, «أرسل لسارة». The app looks for the address in this order:

  1. An email address you typed is used as it is.
  2. The people you have written to (learned from your Sent Items): one match is used. Arabic and Latin spellings of the same name match each other (سارة = Sara).
  3. Otherwise Outlook's address book.
It never guesses. If a name fits several people (two colleagues called Sara, say), the draft keeps the name as you typed it and the assistant lists the candidates and asks you which one you mean.

7. Your writing style

If you set up a writing style in Profile → Writing style, the app itself, not the AI model, adds your greeting and sign-off to every draft and applies your font, size and text direction:

  • one recipient on the To line: your single greeting with their name (“Dear Sara,”);
  • several recipients: your group greeting (“Dear all,”); people on CC do not count;
  • a special person (for example your director) alone on the To line: the greeting you set for them.

Greetings and sign-offs the model writes itself are removed, so you never get two. Arabic greetings join correctly (ل + الأستاذ ← للأستاذ).

8. “Did I reply to this?”

Ask “Did I reply to this email?”, “Did I forward it?” or “Did I take any action on it?”. The assistant checks what Outlook recorded (replied, replied to all, forwarded, and when) and looks for related emails in Sent Items and Drafts, even when you changed the subject, then tells you what you did and when.

9. Follow-ups

Click ⏰ Follow-up above the chat (or ask “What do I need to follow up on?”). You get the requests you sent in the last 10 days that nobody on the To line has answered yet:

  • who you asked, the subject and what you asked for, with ⏳ days waiting;
  • a note if you already chased it or if someone else replied;
  • Open sent email to see what you wrote, and Dismiss to hide an item from future lists (Undo appears for a few seconds; Restore all brings dismissed items back).

The list is worked out from your Sent Items each time you ask. An email counts as a request when it asks for something: “please…”, “could you…”, “let me know…”, a question, or the Arabic forms (نأمل، نرجو، يرجى…). Thank-you notes and announcements are left out.

10. Saved conversations

  • Every chat is saved on this PC. Pick an earlier one from the list at the top of the chat to continue it.
  • ＋ starts a new conversation; 🗑 deletes the current one (only the chat history: emails and drafts are not touched).
  • You can switch the AI provider in Settings in the middle of a conversation; it carries on with the new model.

11. Learning from your sent mail

In the background, every 30 minutes, the app reads your Sent Items (read-only) and keeps on this PC:

  • your past replies next to the emails they answered, so suggested replies match your usual tone and length;
  • the people you write to, so names in your requests turn into the right addresses.

The first run reads the last year of sent mail and can take a minute; later runs only read what is new. Profile → Learning & behaviour shows the progress and has Learn now and Forget learned replies. See Data & privacy for where this is stored.
