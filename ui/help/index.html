Welcome to Inbox Chat

An AI assistant that sits next to your classic Outlook mailbox. Ask about your mail, get a summary and a suggested reply for every email, and have replies, forwards and new emails written for you as drafts.

📝

It never sends

Everything it writes is saved in Outlook's Drafts folder. You read it, change it if you like, and send it from Outlook yourself.

🧠

You choose the AI

A local Ollama model keeps mail on your own network. Cloud models from OpenAI, Anthropic, Google and Moonshot are also supported.

💻

Your data stays on this PC

Settings, conversations and what the app learns are kept in one file on this computer. See Data & privacy.

The window at a glance

AreaWhat it does
Top barTwo status lights: Outlook (connected to your mailbox) and the LLM (the AI model answers). A cloud badge appears when you use a cloud provider. Profile, Settings and Help are on the right.
1 · FoldersYour Outlook folders with unread counts. Click one to list its emails.
2 · MessagesNewest first. Badges show what concerns you: To you CC Mentions you Department Request. Tick For me to hide the rest.
3 · Reading paneThe email as Outlook shows it, with the ✨ Assistant panel on top (summary, what you need to do, suggested reply), then attachments and the earlier messages of the conversation.
4 · AssistantA chat about the open email or your whole mailbox. Answers point to emails as m12; click one to open it.

Drag the thin bars between the panes to make them wider or narrower. The app remembers your layout.

Get started in four steps

  1. Have classic Outlook ready. Inbox Chat works with the classic Outlook desktop app. If Outlook is not running, the app starts it (minimized) and connects when it has loaded.
  2. Choose your AI model in Settings: pick a provider, check the server address, paste an API key for a cloud provider, and choose a model. How to choose →
  3. Tell it about yourself in Profile: how your name appears in emails, your team and its topics, the language you write in and, if you like, your greeting and sign-off. Profile guide →
  4. Open an email and ask. Read the ✨ panel, or type in the chat, for example “Summarize this thread” or “Reply that Wednesday works”. The reply is saved in Drafts. User guide →
Got a settings file? A colleague or another PC can give you a ready-made setup as one file. Load it with Settings → Import from file… and only add your own name and style. Settings files →

What you need

  • Windows 10 or 11 (the WebView2 component is already part of Windows 11 and current Windows 10).
  • Classic Outlook, the desktop app, with your mailbox set up. The new Outlook cannot be used by other programs, so it is not supported.
  • An AI model: Ollama on this PC or a server on your network, or an account with a cloud provider.
  • No installation and no administrator rights: InboxChat.exe runs as it is.

Try it without your mailbox

Start the app with InboxChat.exe --demo to explore a sample mailbox. Demo mode does not touch Outlook and saves nothing.
