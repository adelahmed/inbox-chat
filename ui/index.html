Welcome to Inbox Chat

An assistant for your classic Outlook mailbox. It reads your mail, answers questions about it and saves replies as drafts — it never sends anything.

To start, either load a settings file (from another PC or a colleague), or choose your LLM in Settings and describe yourself in Profile. Help (top right, or F1) explains everything step by step.
